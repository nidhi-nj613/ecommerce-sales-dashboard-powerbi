# POWER BI Sales Project

**Madhav E-Commerce Sales**

An interactive sales analytics dashboard analyzing 5600+ transactions to uncover trends in revenue, profit, customer behavior and payment preferences.

**Objective** : To practice end to end business intelligence : 

- Importing raw sales data.
- Building relevant KPIs.
- Visualizing performance trends across time, category, geography and customer segments.

**Tools Used** : Dax, slicers, charts and cards.

**Key Metrics Tracked** :

- Sum of Amount = ₹438K
- Sum of Profit = ₹37K
- Sum of Quantity = 5615
- Order Value = ₹121k

**Key Insights** : 

- Clothing dominates sales volume by 63% of quantity but printers and bookcase gain the highest profit despite lower unit sales.
- Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh are the top two states by sales, together contributing significantly more than the remaining top-5 states combined.
- COD is still the dominant payment mode i.e. 44%, it shows limited digital payment adoption among this customer base.
